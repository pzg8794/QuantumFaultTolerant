## Official schedule

- **Submission deadline:** Rolling; there is no fixed annual paper deadline.
- **Editorial intake:** New submissions are assessed in regular Editor-in-Chief batches described on the official policies page.
- **Conference registration:** Not applicable; this is a journal submission.
- **Submission account:** IEEE Author Portal/ScholarOne account required; all authors must have ORCIDs.

## Requirements

- Use the official IEEE two-column, single-spaced, 10-point journal template.
- Typical maximum: **16 pages**. Longer manuscripts require advance Editor-in-Chief discussion and cover-letter justification; supplementary material is preferred for excess detail.
- Abstract: **150--250 words**, one paragraph, self-contained, with no abbreviations, footnotes, references, equations, or tables; provide **3--4 keywords**.
- Submit LaTeX or Word source, a complete PDF, and separate figure files when applicable. Do not use JPEG for submitted figures; use accepted formats such as PDF, EPS, PNG, or TIFF.
- All authors must be listed at submission; verify names, order, affiliations, contact details, and ORCIDs.
- Disclose related prior work and explain overlap in the cover letter. Concurrent submission is prohibited.
- Ten published pages are included; the listed mandatory charge is **$220 per page over 10**.
- The journal is hybrid. Open access is optional; if selected, verify the current APC. The 2026 listed APC is **$2,800**.
