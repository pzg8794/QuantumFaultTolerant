## Fit and requirements

- The series explicitly covers quantum-network simulation, benchmarking and testbeds, optimization, resource allocation, architectures/protocols, and quantum switches/routers.
- Prepare with the official IEEE journal template in two-column, single-spaced, 10-point format with one-inch margins.
- Initial manuscript maximum: **13 pages**; revised-paper maximum: **16 pages**. Count title/authors, abstract, index terms, text, equations, figures, tables, appendices, and references.
- Abstract target: **75--200 words**; include no more than **five keywords**.
- Upload through the submission system identified by the live Quantum Series call (currently ScholarOne).
- The journal is hybrid. Open access is optional; if selected, verify the current APC at submission. The 2026 listed APC is **$2,800**.
- The generic JSAC policies page contains inconsistent overlength-charge thresholds in separate sections. Treat **13 pages as the submission maximum**, target **eight published pages where feasible**, and verify the live charge threshold in the portal before submission rather than guessing.
