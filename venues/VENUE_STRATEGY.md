# Venue Strategy

**Last updated:** September 16, 2026

## Current manuscript

The current quantum-routing manuscript was submitted to IEEE ICNP 2026 and is now being revised using the ICNP reviewer feedback.

Following Dan's September 10 venue decision, the primary target is the **IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC) Quantum Series: Quantum Communications and Networking**. The backup is **IEEE Transactions on Networking (TNET)**.

Both venues use rolling submission rather than a conference paper-registration deadline. The JSAC Quantum Series accepts manuscripts at any time and plans March, July, and November issues; those months are publication windows, not submission deadlines. TNET also accepts submissions continuously. We therefore use internal milestones to maintain momentum and give coauthors a predictable review window.

The reviewer-feedback checklist remains the scientific work plan. Venue preparation changes its packaging gates, deliverables, and internal deadlines; it does not replace or broaden the reviewer-driven revision.

## Future ICNP path

We are also considering **IEEE ICNP 2027** for a substantially advanced follow-on version of this research, using the additional work completed during Fall 2026 and, if needed, Spring 2027.

This is **not** a plan to submit the same accepted/published CCWC manuscript to ICNP. An ICNP 2027 submission would need to represent a materially new contribution built from the continuing research program, such as new research questions, algorithms or architecture, larger-scale or different network conditions, deeper theoretical analysis, or substantial new experimental evidence.

Candidate work already in the current roadmap that could contribute to such an advanced version includes:

- medium-scale topology validation (15--20 nodes, 10+ candidate paths);
- diagnosis and ablation of the existing 100-node result;
- deeper allocator-policy analysis;
- stronger threat-to-physics grounding;
- additional context/model/configuration investigation; and
- any new research questions and results that emerge from this semester's work.

## Decision rule

1. Prepare the current reviewer-driven revision for JSAC first and keep a TNET-compatible backup package.
2. Do not submit the manuscript to JSAC and TNET concurrently. Activate the TNET submission only after the JSAC route is withdrawn, declined, or otherwise closed.
3. Treat any later ICNP 2027 submission as a distinct, substantially advanced research contribution rather than a duplicate submission.
4. Reassess ICNP 2027 only if later work produces enough new evidence to define a materially new contribution.
