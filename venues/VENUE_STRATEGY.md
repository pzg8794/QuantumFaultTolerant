# Venue Strategy

**Last updated:** September 2, 2026

## Current manuscript

The current quantum-routing manuscript was submitted to IEEE ICNP 2026 and is now being revised using the ICNP reviewer feedback.

The near-term working resubmission target is **IEEE CCWC 2027**. The current CCWC plan uses the November 6, 2026 full-paper deadline and the internal October 30, 2026 submission-candidate target documented in `venues/ccwc_2027/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Future ICNP path

We are also considering **IEEE ICNP 2027** for a substantially advanced follow-on version of this research, using the additional work completed during Fall 2026 and, if needed, Spring 2027.

This is **not** a plan to submit the same accepted/published CCWC manuscript to ICNP. An ICNP 2027 submission would need to represent a materially new contribution built from the continuing research program, such as new research questions, algorithms or architecture, larger-scale or different network conditions, deeper theoretical analysis, or substantial new experimental evidence.

Candidate work already in the current roadmap that could contribute to such an advanced version includes:

- medium-scale topology validation (15--20 nodes, 10+ candidate paths);
- diagnosis and ablation of the existing 100-node result;
- deeper allocator-policy analysis;
- stronger threat-to-physics grounding;
- additional context/model/configuration investigation; and
- any new research questions and results that emerge from this semester's work.

## Decision rule

1. Continue the current reviewer-driven revision on the CCWC 2027 working timeline unless the coauthors choose a different near-term venue.
2. Treat any later ICNP 2027 submission as a distinct, substantially advanced research contribution rather than a duplicate resubmission of an accepted conference paper.
3. Reassess the ICNP 2027 option once the Fall 2026 experimental work produces enough new evidence to define a clear new contribution.
