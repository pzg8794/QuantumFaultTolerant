# IEEE CCWC 2027 Submission Checklist

**Working venue target:** IEEE CCWC 2027

**Official full-paper deadline:** November 6, 2026

**Internal submission-candidate deadline:** October 30, 2026

This checklist converts the live venue requirements into dated project gates
and connects them to the [reviewer-feedback tasks](../../updates/FEEDBACK_TASKS.md).
IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking remains the longer-form fallback; it is not
mixed into this conference checklist.

## Current Official Requirements

- Submission is electronic through EDAS.
- Review manuscripts must be double-blind: remove author names, affiliations,
  IEEE grades, funding references, and acknowledgments.
- Use the IEEE conference manuscript template.
- Regular papers are limited to 7 pages; up to 3 additional pages are allowed
  for an extra charge.
- Manuscripts must report original, unpublished work and are checked for
  plagiarism/self-plagiarism.
- The conference is scheduled as an in-person event in Las Vegas, January
  4--6, 2027.

Official sources:

- [CCWC 2027 Call for Papers](https://ieee-ccwc.org/call-for-papers/)
- [CCWC 2027 Submission Instructions](https://ieee-ccwc.org/submissions/)

## Manuscript Compliance

- [ ] IEEE conference template is used without unauthorized margin, font, or
  spacing changes.
- [ ] The team explicitly chooses either the 7-page regular-paper target or an
  8--10-page paid-extra-page submission before compression begins.
- [ ] Author names, affiliations, IEEE grades, funding, acknowledgments, and
  identifying PDF metadata are absent from the review build.
- [ ] The anonymous artifact link and self-citations do not reveal author or
  institutional identity.
- [ ] The title, abstract, keywords, and manuscript uploaded to EDAS agree.
- [ ] Figures and tables remain readable at final two-column size.
- [ ] References, cross-references, equations, and algorithms compile without
  unresolved markers.
- [ ] No reviewer comments, TODOs, highlights, internal notes, or tracked-change
  artifacts render in the PDF.
- [ ] Originality and prior-paper overlap are reviewed before submission.

## Evidence and Submission Readiness

- [ ] Every central claim maps to a validated figure, table, notebook result, or
  documented dataset query.
- [ ] Medium-scale and 100-node results are reproducible from retained
  configurations, logs, and analysis code.
- [ ] The anonymous repository contains only publication-safe code, data, and
  documentation.
- [ ] The final PDF opens correctly and its page count matches the chosen page
  strategy.
- [ ] EDAS upload completes and the submission record is checked before the
  official deadline.
