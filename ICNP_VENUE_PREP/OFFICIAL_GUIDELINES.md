# Official ICNP 2026 Submission Guidelines

Source: https://icnp26.cs.ucr.edu/submission.html

## Format and length

- Papers must be written in English.
- Main paper body must be no more than 10 pages in double-column format.
- The 10-page limit includes figures and tables.
- References and appendices may use unlimited pages.
- Longer submissions will not be reviewed.
- The abstract must be fewer than 250 words.

## Page and template requirements

- Papers must be formatted for US Letter paper: 8.5 in by 11 in.
- Papers must use the IEEE formatting requirements.
- LaTeX submissions must use the `conference` option.
- The template should be used as-is.
- Narrower line spacing or margins than the template defines may cause rejection without review.

## PDF requirements

- Submit as PDF.
- PDF must be Acrobat-compatible.
- Avoid special characters and non-standard fonts.
- A PDF checker is run on all submitted papers.
- Non-conformant papers can be flagged and rejected without review if not fixed before the deadline.
- The PDF must display and print exactly as submitted using standard tools and printers.
- Strong recommendation: use only standard fonts.

## Double-blind review requirements

- Remove author names and affiliations from the submitted PDF.
- The checklist explicitly requires author names and affiliations removed for double-blind review.
- The blind version should also remove or anonymize links, acknowledgments, metadata, and comments that identify the authors.

## Figure and plot requirements

The venue does not require grayscale-only figures. The official requirement is that papers must print well on black-and-white printers, especially plots and graphs.

Practical rule for this project:

```text
Color is allowed, but color cannot be the only visual distinction.
Every plot/graph should also use redundant cues such as line style, marker shape, fill shade, direct labels, threshold lines, or grouping.
```

This corrects the earlier mistaken shorthand that treated the venue as if it required grayscale-only figures.

## Submission checklist

Before submission, verify that:

- the official ICNP 2026 submission page is used;
- the paper has a title;
- authors, emails, and affiliations are entered in the submission system;
- the uploaded PDF complies with IEEE formatting;
- the PDF has no more than 10 main-body pages excluding references;
- the PDF uses US Letter, 10-point font, and IEEE conference style;
- author names and affiliations are removed from the PDF for review;
- the abstract uploaded to the system is no more than 250 words;
- all PC conflicts are indicated.
