\subsection{Additional Claim Support}
\label{app:checkpoint_claim_support}

The following figures provide supporting views for claims that are central to the paper but too detailed for the main Results flow. They extend the main-body evidence in \Cref{fig:cross_testbed_confirmation,fig:capacity_paradox,fig:deployment_guidance} by showing contextual-versus-EXP3 Oracle-gap behavior, the retained context-capacity interaction, and threat-conditioned deployment structure in more detail. \Cref{fig:appendix_g8_advanced_synthesis} now carries the replay-configuration sensitivity view in Panel~(D), replacing the duplicate context-capacity panel that previously appeared in the grouped synthesis figure.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\textbf{Contextual vs. EXP3 Oracle Gap}\\[-0.0em]
\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/icnp/ICNP-CODE-032_g9_network_gap_analysis_panel_c_oracle_gap_context_vs_exp3_by_scenario_c.png}
\caption{The \texttt{Oracle} gap is the percentage-point distance between
achieved efficiency and the \texttt{Oracle} baseline, shown across scenario and
capacity settings. Contextual policies produce lower \texttt{Oracle} gaps than
EXP3-style baselines, showing that path and context information reduce
efficiency loss under stochastic, structured, and adaptive disruption. This
supports the RQ1/RQ2 claim that context-aware routing remains more stable than
adversarial-first EXP3-style designs as disruption progresses from stochastic
noise to adaptive interference.}
