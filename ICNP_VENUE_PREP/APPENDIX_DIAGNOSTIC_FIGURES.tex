They extend the main-body evidence in \Cref{fig:cross_testbed_confirmation,fig:capacity_paradox,fig:deployment_guidance} by showing contextual-versus-EXP3 Oracle-gap behavior, capacity sensitivity, and threat-conditioned deployment structure in more detail.

\caption{The \texttt{Oracle} gap is the percentage-point distance between
achieved efficiency and the \texttt{Oracle} baseline, shown across scenario and

efficiency loss under stochastic, structured, and adaptive disruption. This
supports the RQ1/RQ2 claim that context-aware routing remains more stable than
adversarial-first EXP3-style designs as disruption progresses from stochastic

\subsection{Grouped Synthesis Diagnostics}
\label{app:grouped_synthesis_diagnostics}

The grouped synthesis figure below remains in the appendix because it combines multiple diagnostic views without repeating the specific G9 panels now used in the main body. \Cref{fig:appendix_g8_advanced_synthesis} serves as the appendix-side reference point when readers want one grouped view of how the detailed diagnostics connect.

\begin{figure*}[ht!]
\centering
\textbf{Advanced Synthesis Diagnostics}\\[-0.0em]
\includegraphics[width=0.98\textwidth]{figures/icnp/ICNP-CODE-024_g8_advanced_4panel_grouped_full_figure.png}
\caption{Panel~(A) shows that contextual and pursuit--neural policies
concentrate at lower \texttt{Oracle} gaps, indicating lower efficiency loss than
weaker baselines. Panel~(B) shows the capacity-paradox pattern: intermediate
replay scale produces the weakest robustness, while larger scale recovers
performance in this threat configuration. Panel~(C) shows that allocator choice
changes both average efficiency and tail behavior, making allocation policy part
of the robustness mechanism. Panel~(D) shows that the pursuit--neural advantage
persists across external testbeds even as larger or more constrained topologies
reduce \texttt{Oracle} separation and alter second-tier rankings. Together, the
panels show that routing robustness is governed by the interaction among policy
family, replay/capacity setting, allocator choice, and testbed scale.}

\label{fig:appendix_g8_advanced_synthesis}
\end{figure*}
